# FRAME — no work on an unspecified task

**The problem.** The model can produce a lot of useful-looking work before the
task is specified. If the operator does not choose the frame, the model's
defaults choose it.

**The rule.** Before deep work, require a confirmed short FRAME: deliverable ·
success criterion · stop condition. Require full FRAME when the task is
ambiguous, costly to reverse, likely to expand, public, or governed by
conflicting criteria. Full FRAME adds forbidden outcome · suspected bottleneck ·
cheapest useful test · hard constraints (the forbidden region). The model may
draft the frame, but it is not accepted until the operator confirms it.
Template: [`state/taskcard.md`](../state/taskcard.md).

**Skipped, it fails as:** fluent work on a task nobody chose. The output may be
good, but it answers a different problem.

**Failure pattern** *(canon: [`ENGINE`](../docs/ENGINE.md) §3 — compliance
theater; the deeper residual is framing error)*. A card that never constrains
the work: the success criterion is vague, the stop condition is movable, or the
forbidden outcome is something nobody was going to do anyway.
**The test:** name what the frame ruled out. If it ruled out nothing, the card
was a label, not a boundary.

**Engine line.** The operator keeps the choice of task, criteria, and stop
condition. → [`docs/ENGINE.md`](../docs/ENGINE.md)
