# Task card — the FRAME record

One card per deep task, confirmed before work starts. The model may draft the
card; the operator accepts or revises it. Use short FRAME by default and full
FRAME when the task is ambiguous, costly to reverse, likely to expand, public,
or governed by conflicting criteria.

---

## Worked example — fictional

**Task:** landing copy — rewrite before launch?
**Deliverable:** a go/no-go decision for me, tonight, on rewriting the landing page before the v0.1 launch.
**Success criterion:** decision made with at least one datum that isn't my own taste.
**Stop condition:** five responses or 30 minutes, whichever comes first — then decide.

**Full FRAME trigger:** public launch copy; easy to expand into a rewrite.
**Forbidden outcome:** a rewrite that moves the launch date; polishing used as a way to avoid shipping.
**Suspected bottleneck:** not the copy — the absence of any conversion signal in either direction.
**Cheapest useful test:** show both versions to five people in the target segment; count who can repeat the pitch back.
**Hard constraints (forbidden region):** do not change positioning until the decision is made.
