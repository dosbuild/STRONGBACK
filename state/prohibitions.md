# Prohibitions — the forbidden trades

Not morals. Engineering constraints: trades that pay off in the moment and
break the loop over a long horizon. The kernel set ships with the protocol
because the loop depends on it. Personal entries go underneath; they should
come from your own closes, not from borrowed slogans.

Shape: the trade · the mechanism it destroys · the tell that erosion has started.

---

**1. Understanding → throughput.** Shipping a consequential claim whose basis, assumptions, and likely failure conditions the operator cannot explain.
Destroys: accountability — the operator has no usable basis for defending, extending, or noticing the claim breaking.
Tell: the answer can be pasted, but not explained.

**2. Error exposure → convenience.** Counting a check that cannot name a plausible error in the original process it could expose.
Destroys: ATTACK — verification becomes repetition with a new label.
Tell: "I double-checked" means you re-read it or asked for a paraphrase.

**3. Learning → speed.** Closing a deep task with no logged delta.
Destroys: compounding — the work repeats at full price forever.
Tell: your decisions log reads as a diary of activity, not a history of changed beliefs.

**4. Log integrity → ego.** Editing a logged commitment or scale after the outcome, or deleting a miss.
Destroys: calibration — the record no longer shows what the operator believed before seeing the result.
Tell: your hit rate looks better than you feel.

**5. Externalized state → familiarity.** Letting the canonical state of a task live in your head.
Destroys: the compute split — the head is a cache; the moment it becomes the source of truth, variables start dropping again.
Tell: you can't hand the task to the model, or a person, without an hour of re-explaining.

The last two are #4 at repository scale — record integrity. A protocol that
measures itself in public inherits the same constraint as the private log:

**6. Evidence → story.** Presenting a fictional example as repository history.
Destroys: evidence posture — examples become fake data.
Tell: a worked example lacks a visible fictional label.

**7. Interest → adoption.** Calling stars, forks, visits, or saves "installs."
Destroys: measurement — attention gets counted as use.
Tell: a metric cannot prove anyone ran a gate, but the copy says they did.
