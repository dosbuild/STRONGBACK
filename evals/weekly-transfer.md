# Weekly transfer test — two runs, pass/fail

Run both once a week. Log each in `state/predictions.log` and record fails in
`state/model-of-me.md`. These runs target the two failures that cannot be felt
from inside: gains that live in the file, and gains that live in familiar
domains. Canonical failure registry: [`docs/ENGINE.md`](../docs/ENGINE.md) §3.

## Test 1 — Cold domain

Pick a domain you haven't worked in this quarter. One real task, full loop,
about an hour.

**PASS:** the FRAME you confirmed named a suspected bottleneck, and that
bottleneck survived ATTACK — the attack named a real error mechanism, and the
bottleneck either held or broke and was replaced by a sharper one.
**FAIL:** the bottleneck could not be named without the model choosing it, or
the attack could not say what error it was hunting. The ritual may have run;
the substance didn't.

This test scores substance, not compliance — whether you logged on time is
already visible in the log itself. The question it asks: did the loop produce
structure in a domain you brought no structure to?

Caveat, owned: in a cold domain you don't yet know what a good check looks
like — the domain's failure modes are exactly what you lack — so the gates are
weakest where the load on them is highest. A cold PASS is real but noisier
evidence than a warm one (ENGINE §5).

A FAIL means your gains so far are domain libraries, not portable transfer.
Real, useful, and local. Keep running; retest in four weeks.

## Test 2 — Bare (no STRONGBACK)

One deep task in a plain session, file absent. No gates, no enforcement.
Work as you now naturally work.

**PASS:** unprompted, you still committed an expectation and ran at least one
check that named a plausible error.
**FAIL:** either was missing.

A FAIL means current performance is system-dependent — the system got sharper,
not (yet) you. Legitimate when known and logged; corrosive when misattributed.

Why introspection can't replace this run: a collapsed CLOSE breaks the same
faculty that would report it, so "I'm still careful" is produced by the broken
instrument. The bare run is the one observation that doesn't route through it
(ENGINE §5).
